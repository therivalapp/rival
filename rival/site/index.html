Social fitness

Fitness is better shared.

How it works

Every activity counts.

Teams

Small groups of friends, clubs or training partners. Activities, messages and challenges, shared only with the people in the team.

Effort

One score for every kind of activity, from a 5 km run to a heavy lifting day, so different sports can be compared fairly.

Events

Marathons, triathlons, HYROX and competitions, with finish times, splits, placings and photos on one page.

Goals

Distance, elevation, best times and lifts, tracked from the activities already recorded.

Connected devices

Activities from the watch.

Activities arrive from Apple Health, RIVAL's own GPS recording and, once connected, Garmin. Each one is shown with the device that recorded it.

Data from connected devices is used only to record activities, score Effort and show them to the teams each member chooses. It is never sold and never used for advertising. Disconnecting a device stops new data arriving, and deleting an account deletes its data. The privacy policy has the details.
