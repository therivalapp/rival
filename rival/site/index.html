How it works

Every activity counts.

Teams

Small groups of friends, clubs or training partners. Activities, messages and challenges, shared only with the people in the team.

Effort

One score for every kind of activity, from a 5 km run to a heavy lifting day, so different sports can be compared fairly.

Recognition

Teammates react to every activity and encourage each other, so effort is seen and recognised.

Challenges

Team goals and challenges worked toward together, with every member's part shown.

Events

Marathons, triathlons, HYROX and competitions, with finish times, splits, placings and photos on one page.

Goals

Distance, elevation, best times and lifts, tracked from the activities already recorded.

Connected devices

Activities from the watch.

Activities are recorded with RIVAL's own GPS, and soon come in from Apple Health on iPhone. Each one is shown with the device that recorded it.

Activity data is shared only with the teams each member chooses, and is never sold. The privacy policy has the details.

Free to use

Recognition is never for sale.

Recording activities, Effort, ranks, teams and recognition are free and stay free, with no advertising.
