How it works

Every team you train with, in one app.

Run club, gym, training partners, family. Join each team, and share every activity with the teams you choose.

Teams

All your teams, one feed.

Each team has its own podium, chat and challenge. The All Teams Feed brings them together, so one activity is seen by every team it's shared with.

Effort

One score for every sport.

A 5 km run, a heavy lifting day and a triathlon all earn Effort, so every kind of training counts.

Recognition

Effort that gets seen.

Teammates give Respect on every activity, and the weekly podium celebrates who put in the most.

Challenges

Team targets, with every member's part shown.

Events

Race days with finish times, splits and photos.

Goals

Distance, elevation, best times and lifts.

Connected devices

Wherever an activity is recorded.

Record with RIVAL's own GPS. Apple Health on iPhone arrives with the beta, and more watches and devices are being connected. Every activity shows the device that recorded it.

Your data

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Download or delete all of it, any time.
