RIVAL

A rival brings out your best.

A rival isn't someone you're against. It's someone who inspires you to be better, and who you inspire in return. Showing up is easier when your team is with you.

How it works

Every team, in one app.

Run club, gym, friends, family. Join each team, and share every activity with the teams you choose.

Teams

All your teams, one feed.

Each team has its own podium, chat and challenge. The All Teams Feed brings them together, so one activity is seen by every team it's shared with.

Effort

One score for every sport.

Walk, swim, yoga, a heavy lift, a round of golf or a day on the slopes. Every activity earns Effort, so every kind of movement counts.

Recognition

Effort that gets seen.

Any activity can earn Respect from teammates, and the weekly podium celebrates who put in the most effort.

Challenges

One team target, and every member's effort counts towards it.

Events

Work towards an event with your teammates, then celebrate every finish together.

Goals

Choose what matters to you this year, and watch every activity move you closer.

Connected devices

Wherever an activity is recorded.

Record with RIVAL's own GPS. Apple Health on iPhone arrives with the beta, and more watches and devices are being connected. Every activity shows the device that recorded it.

Bring your past activities with you, so your lifetime Effort and total hours earned are complete from day one.
