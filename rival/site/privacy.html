Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

RIVAL is a social fitness app. You log activities, earn Effort, and share them with the teams you join. This policy explains what RIVAL collects, who can see it, and how to remove it. RIVAL collects only what it needs to run, does not sell personal information, and does not show advertising. RIVAL is run by its founder, Ricky Jackson-Lewis, from British Columbia, Canada, who is responsible for this policy and the information RIVAL holds.

What RIVAL collects

Account. Email address, name, date of birth, time zone, optional username, profile photo, cover photo and personal quote. Your date of birth is used only to confirm your age and is never shown to other members.

Activities. Each activity you add: type, date and time, duration, distance, elevation, exercises, sets and weights, notes, Effort, personal bests, goals, events and finish times. Activities come from manual entry, workout scanning, archive import and RIVAL's own GPS recording, and, once available, Apple Health on iPhone and Garmin.

Routes. When an activity has a GPS route, RIVAL stores a simplified outline of it. Teammates see route maps only if you turn on route sharing.

Photos and videos. Files you attach to activities, set as a cover, or upload for workout scanning.

Team activity. Posts, messages, comments, reactions (Respect and Inspired), notes, challenges and planned activities you post in your teams, encouragements you send, training partners you tag, events you are interested in, and who invited you to a team.

Device token. A push-notification token, if you allow notifications.

Usage. RIVAL does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The services that host RIVAL keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and request times, for security and to fix faults. RIVAL records when you last used the app.

RIVAL does not collect contacts or advertising identifiers.

Apple Health

This applies once RIVAL's iPhone app is available. On iPhone, you can let RIVAL read your workouts from Apple Health. RIVAL reads workout type, time, duration, distance, elevation, energy and route, and only after you allow it in the Health permission screen. Health data is used only to record your activities, score Effort and show them to your teams. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never given to data brokers, and never stored in iCloud. RIVAL does not write anything to Apple Health. You can turn access off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

GPS recording

When you record an activity in RIVAL, the app uses your location only while that activity is recording, to measure distance and draw the route. Your live location is never shared.

Garmin Connect

This applies once Garmin connection is available. If you connect a Garmin account, RIVAL receives the activities your Garmin device records: activity type, start time, duration, distance, elevation, heart rate, pace and splits, route, and the exercises, sets, repetitions and weights of strength activities. RIVAL does not request sleep, stress, steps or other wellness data.

Garmin data is used only to record your activities, score Effort and show them to the teams you choose. Activities from Garmin are marked with the device that recorded them. RIVAL never sells Garmin data, never uses it for advertising and never shares it with anyone other than the service providers that run RIVAL. Disconnecting Garmin stops new activities arriving, and deleting your account deletes the Garmin data RIVAL holds.

Imports from other apps

You can import your history from an archive file, from RIVAL or another fitness app. The import reads the activities, routes, photos and videos in the file you choose. Nothing else on your device is read.

AI features

Some features send content to Anthropic, an AI provider, to produce a result:

  • Workout scanning sends the photo you choose, such as a whiteboard or gym screen, to read the workout details from it.
  • Placings scanning sends the results photo you choose, to read finish places from it.
  • Find events sends the event name you search for.
  • Team crests send the team's name. The crest image is made by fal.ai, and Anthropic reads its lettering back to check the name is spelled correctly.

These providers process the content only to return the result. RIVAL does not send your activity history to them or use your content to train AI models. If a photo includes other people, make sure they are happy for it to be uploaded.

Who can see your information

  • Your teammates see your name, photos, activities, Effort, rank, profile, reactions, comments and messages in the teams you join. RIVAL asks for your agreement before your activities are shared with any team.
  • Public teams show their name, crest and member count in Discover. Joining one needs a member or an admin to confirm.
  • People you tag in an activity see it, and get their own copy if they confirm.
  • Events you add, and who is interested in them, can be seen by all members, so others can join them.
  • Images you share go wherever you send them.
  • Service providers run RIVAL on its behalf: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (web hosting), Cloudflare (domain and email forwarding), Anthropic and fal.ai (the AI features above), Expo (push notifications) and Resend (account emails).

RIVAL never sells personal information and does not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

Where data is stored

RIVAL's data is stored in the United States, with Supabase in Oregon. Some of the service providers listed above process it in the United States or other countries, under their own privacy and security commitments.

Beta sign-ups

If you join the beta list on therivalapp.com, RIVAL keeps your name, email address and country (with province, in Canada), only to send your beta invite and to check the beta is open where you live. It is not added to a mailing list or used for anything else, and it is deleted when the beta opens to everyone, or sooner if you ask at hello@therivalapp.com.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept while your account is open.
  • You can delete any activity, photo, goal or event in the app.
  • Settings, Account, Download data saves a copy of your information.
  • Settings, Account, Delete account offers a copy of your activities, photos and videos, then emails you a final confirmation. Once you confirm, your account and its data are permanently deleted. Teams you created pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the only one.
  • Deleted information can remain in backups for up to 30 days before it is removed.
  • If anything goes wrong, contact hello@therivalapp.com and the deletion will be completed within 14 days.

Security

Data is stored with Supabase. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access rules in the database limit each person to the data they are allowed to see. RIVAL looks at personal information only to run the service, help members who ask for support, and fix faults. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, RIVAL will tell the people affected and the privacy regulators where the law requires it.

Children

RIVAL is not for children under 13, or under the minimum age where you live, and does not knowingly collect their information. Sign-up asks for a date of birth and does not create accounts for anyone under 13. If a child's account is found, it is deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act 1988, New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 and the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. Contact hello@therivalapp.com, and RIVAL will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, RIVAL will say so in the app and update the date above. If a company takes over running RIVAL, your information passes to it under this policy, and you will be told.
