Past weeks that still feel alive

Today a past week is a title, Effort and three numbers, with nothing of the week itself. Each option gives it back something that happened. Example: Oct 5 – 11, 95.7 Effort, 2 activities, a run on Wednesday and Friday.

Past weeks get the date kicker and the day columns, exactly like this week, so they read as the same thing, just finished. Folded it's header and columns; open adds the numbers.
