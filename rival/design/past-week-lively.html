Past weeks, with some life in them

The numbers lead, not the days. What makes them interesting is context: how the week compares to your others, what it was made of, and a picture of it. Example: Week 41, Oct 5 – 11. 4 h 12 m, 3 activities, 21.6 km, 340 m climbing, 312.4 Effort, your 3rd strongest week this year.
