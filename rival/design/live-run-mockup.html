After Start
The last-time numbers stay on the Start screen, before you begin. Once the clock starts, the screen shows this run. Swipe sideways for Splits and Map, as on Strava.
1 · Stats (swipe 1 of 3)
Distance is the hero, as on Nike Run Club, because it is what runners watch. Under it: time, pace for the current kilometre, and heart rate. The second row is average pace, elevation gain and calories. Capture The Moment stays as a quiet reminder.
