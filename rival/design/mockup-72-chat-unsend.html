Mockup 72 · Unsend a message
Like Messenger: hold your own message, Unsend, confirm. It is removed for everyone and leaves a short note in its place.
1 · HOLD YOUR MESSAGE
Unsend sits below Reply and Copy, in its own group. Only on your own messages.
2 · CONFIRM
The app's usual confirm pop-up.
3 · AFTER
You see "You unsent a message". Teammates see "Ricky unsent a message". A reply to it shows "Message unsent".
