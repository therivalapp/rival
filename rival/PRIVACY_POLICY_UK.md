## United Kingdom

If you live in the UK, these details also apply under the UK GDPR.

**Who is responsible.** Ricky Jackson-Lewis, who runs RIVAL from British
Columbia, Canada, is the controller of your information:
**hello@therivalapp.com**. RIVAL's representative in the UK is
[REPRESENTATIVE NAME, ADDRESS AND EMAIL].

**Why RIVAL uses your information:**

- **To provide RIVAL.** Your account, activities, Effort, teams, messages and
  the features you choose to use are needed to run the service you signed up
  for.
- **With your consent.** Sharing activities with a team, recording your
  location during an activity, and connecting Apple Health or Garmin. Health
  information from a device, such as heart rate, is used only with your
  explicit consent, given when you connect it. You can withdraw consent at any
  time in the app or on your device, without affecting anything done before.
- **To keep RIVAL safe and working.** Technical logs, security checks and
  fixing faults are RIVAL's legitimate interests.
- **When the law requires it.**

**Transfers.** RIVAL is run from Canada, which the UK recognises as protecting
personal information. RIVAL's data is stored in the United States, and service
providers may process it in other countries. Those transfers are protected by
the providers' data processing agreements, using the UK's approved contract
terms or the UK-US data bridge.

**Your rights.** You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your
information, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to withdraw consent.
RIVAL makes no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or
similarly significant effects. If you are unhappy with how your information is
handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at
**ico.org.uk**.
